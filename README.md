## 🔬 About Me

I'm a Materials and Welding Engineer with a strong focus on **Wire-Arc Additive Manufacturing (WAAM)**, **finite element simulation**, and **mechanical properties analysis**.

My research centers on understanding how manufacturing parameters affect residual stress, microstructure, and performance in additively manufactured steel components — combining experimental work with thermal-mechanical FEM modeling in ANSYS APDL.

I also hold a B.Sc. in Safety & Inspection Engineering, where I developed expertise in **Risk-Based Inspection (RBI)** and damage mechanism assessment for industrial equipment.

---

## 🧪 Research Highlights

- **WAAM Simulation** — Thermal-mechanical FEM modeling of residual stress in low-alloy steel fabricated by Wire-Arc Additive Manufacturing (ANSYS APDL)
- **Hydrogen Cracking** — Quantifying the relationship between hydrogen content and strain in API 5L X70 steel
- **Post-Processing Effects** — Investigating how post-processing influences the mechanical properties of WAAM components
- **RBI Program** — Developed a full Risk-Based Inspection program for a distillation tower unit using Damage Plus software
